About this variety

The original aquarium neocaridina. Red Cherry shrimp were among the first colour variants developed, and they've earned their status as the go-to beginner shrimp. Cherry-grade individuals show solid red on most of the body, while higher grades - Sakura and Fire Red - display deeper, more uniform colour with red on the legs and underbelly. Hardy, prolific, and peaceful, they're an excellent starting point for anyone new to the hobby and a lively addition to any planted tank.
