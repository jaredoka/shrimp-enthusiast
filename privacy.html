Privacy Policy

What we collect

Shrimp Enthusiast is a simple static website with no user accounts, no server-side processing, and no database. We do not collect personal information through forms or tracking scripts on this site.

When you place an order via WhatsApp checkout, you voluntarily share your name, phone number, and delivery details directly through WhatsApp. This information is used solely to fulfil your order and is not stored in any separate system.

Local storage

This website uses your browser's localStorage to save your shopping cart contents. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never sent to any server. You can clear it at any time by emptying your browser's site data or clearing your cart on the site.

Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter and Lora typefaces. Google may collect basic connection data (such as your IP address) when fonts are requested. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • WhatsApp — used for order checkout and customer communication. When you click the checkout button, your order details are sent to our WhatsApp number. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to all messages.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages visited, time on site, general location). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymised usage data. No personally identifiable information is collected. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Cookies & tracking

This website uses cookies set by Google Analytics to collect anonymised usage data such as pages visited and session duration. No advertising pixels or retargeting tools are used. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please reach out via WhatsApp.
