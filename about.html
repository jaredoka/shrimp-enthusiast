Behind Shrimp Enthusiast

Our story

A hobbyist at heart

Shrimp Enthusiast started the way most hobbyist operations do - with one tank, a colony of Red Cherry shrimp, and a growing obsession with neocaridinas. What began as a personal hobby gradually became something more: a small breeding setup dedicated to producing healthy, vibrant shrimp for fellow enthusiasts right here in Brunei.

Every variety we sell has been raised in our own tanks, observed through full breeding cycles, and selected for health and colour before it ever reaches a new home. We know every colony personally - because we set them up ourselves.

"Our goal is to help hobbyists understand what it takes to build a thriving environment for their shrimp. When the tank is right, everything else follows."
Shrimp Enthusiast
Why buy local

Less stress, better shrimp

🤝

Support local hobbyists

The aquarium hobby in Brunei grows stronger when we support each other. Whether you get your shrimp from us or from another local fish shop, buying locally keeps the community alive and gives you access to healthier livestock and real advice from people who care.

💧

Raised in Brunei water

Our shrimp are bred and raised in local water conditions. That means less acclimatisation stress and a much smoother transition into your Brunei-based tank - a small detail that makes a real difference in survival and breeding.

📦

No shipping stress

Shrimp bought online spend days sealed in a bag - temperature swings, oxygen depletion, stress hormones. When you buy from us locally, your shrimp go from our tank to yours in the same day, calm and ready to settle in.

The setup

Behind the scenes

We run dedicated breeding tanks for each variety - no mixing, no cross-contamination. Each tank is heavily planted with christmas moss, anubias, bucephalandra, and vallisneria to provide hiding spots, grazing surfaces, and the biological stability shrimp thrive in.

Shrimp are fed a varied diet of blanched vegetables, spirulina wafers, and quality commercial pellets. We cull regularly for colour and vitality, so what goes out is genuinely representative of each colony at its best.
